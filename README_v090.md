# DUALORA 0.9.0 — Real FPS monitor (PresentMon CSV)\n\nChoose a CSV recorded by Intel PresentMon, with MsBetweenPresents, MsBetweenDisplayChange or FrameTime columns. A frametime chart and calculated average FPS, 1% low and stutter count update every two seconds as the CSV is refreshed. No fake FPS, no invasive GPU hooks; relies on a separate PresentMon recording. The 1% low is derived from the slowest 1% frame times. Hardware gaming validation is still required.\n
